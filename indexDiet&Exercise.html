1. Aerobic Exercise

• Activities like brisk walking, running, stationary biking, and swimming are effective for burning calories.
• At least 30 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic exercise daily is recommended for weight loss; more time leads to better results (Donnelly et al., 2004).

2. High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT)

• HIIT is superior as it burns more calories in less time, with a calorie-burning effect that can continue up to 24 hours post-exercise (Haines & Mansi, 2023).
• Involves alternating between intense activity (e.g., sprints) and short rest periods.

3. Strength Training

• Important for preserving muscle mass during weight loss, which helps increase basal metabolism.
• Donnelly et al. (2004) suggest that strength training supports long-term metabolic health.

4. Behavioral Strategies

• Goal setting, activity tracking, and social support enhance adherence to long-term exercise routines.

Dietary Patterns

Sustainable healthy diets should ensure adequate macro/micronutrients, energy balance, and support metabolic health. The following diets are evidence-based:

1. Mediterranean Diet

• Focuses on fruits, vegetables, whole grains, nuts, olive oil, fish, and moderate dairy/meat.
• Clinical Trial (PREDIMED-Plus): Energy-reduced version + physical activity led to significant weight loss (~3.9 kg; p < 0.01) and improved cardiometabolic risk via gut microbiota modulation.
• Meta-analysis: Average BMI reduction of ~0.71 kg/m², waist reduction ~1.69 cm, plus improved blood pressure and glycemic control.

2. DASH Diet

• Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension. Originally to reduce blood pressure, also aids in weight loss.
• 16-week RCT (post-bariatric patients): Calorie-limited DASH led to better BMI, fat and mass reduction.
• 8-week Triple-blind RCT (children with overweight/obesity): Reduced body weight, waist circumference, BMI, WWI, and BRI — even without specific calorie restriction.

3. Intermittent Fasting (IF)

• Includes Alternate-Day Fasting (ADF), Time-Restricted Eating (TRE), and 5:2 Diet.
• 6-month RCT (3 arms, adults > 30 years): Average weight loss –2.05 kg, BMI –0.73 kg/m²; significant lean mass loss.
• Umbrella Review: TRE linked to fat/weight loss and better glucose control.
• 5:2 Diet shown to reduce LDL cholesterol.
